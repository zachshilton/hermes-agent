# Accounts, posting, and how long to run a test

## Account setup
- **Gmail or Yahoo. Never Outlook** — stated in two separate videos; "every single
  account just gets sniped", tried "like four times". Hotmail "probably a stretch".
- **Warm-up: no more than two days.** Applies to both niche and wow products.
  - Niche product: scroll ONLY that niche's content.
  - Wow/broad product: just doom-scroll normally and like what is already viral.
- **Never warm up with generic "Amazon finds" content** — "you're just a burner
  magnet".
- Named scam: **bulkaccountsby.com** — reportedly reset passwords and resold ~100
  of his aged accounts.

## What to expect from accounts
His taxonomy: **dead** (properly warmed, still never pops — "not always in your
control"), **slowburn**, **normal**, and **personal-branding** accounts.

Expected spread: **~10% dead, ~40% slowburn** (need sustained daily posting of
genuinely different creatives), the rest can pop on the first or second post.
On TikTok, roughly a 50/50 split between slowburn and normal.

- **TikTok is faster; Instagram is slowburn but converts far better.**
  *"90% of the money in the organic industry is made on Instagram."*
- A TikTok slowburn account takes roughly a week to its first viral video.

## The Niche Recycle Method
Keep **one account per niche** and test multiple products on it rather than
deleting or rebranding. Order ~3 niche-adjacent products and speed-test all three
on that one niche-locked account — "the account's not going to die" because the
niche never changes.

Corroborated independently: pivoting to a new product **within the same niche on an
already-warm account beats starting fresh**; a cold or off-niche pivot kills reach.

- **Archive old posts, never delete** — deleting reportedly hurts the account on
  Instagram. Rebrand bio and picture instead.
- One niche per account. Jumping car -> watch -> lighter reads as inconsistent.

## Posting
- **3 videos/day, consistently, for multiple weeks.**
- But **2-3 higher-quality videos a day beats 4 lower-quality ones.**
- **Volume does not force virality:** *"you can't post like 80 times in a day and
  then that's going to negate the luck of going viral."*

## How long to run a test
- **Two products roughly every 10-13 days**, with research running in parallel.
  Order the NEXT product on day 1, never after the current test ends.
- **Be testing within 5 days maximum** of finding a product. A slow dropshipper
  takes ~20 days.
- **Run 2-3 products at a time**, each for a maximum of a week and a half to two
  weeks.
- **Concept testing:** test a concept at least TWICE with tweaks. Not viral by the
  3rd attempt, change a lot. Not viral by the 5th, drop it. Never repost the same
  concept again.
- **7 to 10 videos** before you should expect to see an outlier at all.

## Do not kill a product until all four "ample" levers are tried
1. **Ample concepts** — the format may be failing, not the product.
2. **Ample angles** — several ICPs on one product, each for a week or two.
3. **Ample offers** — healthy traffic and no sales means fix price/offer first.
4. **Ample products** — once an account is healthy, test several in that niche.

Top sellers have tested "hundreds if not over a thousand" products. Testing 4-5
and stopping is far too few. Testing "a product a month" is called bottom-feeder.
