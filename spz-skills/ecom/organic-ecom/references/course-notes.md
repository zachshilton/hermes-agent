## What to write, and why it is not a summary

A summary of a lesson is close to worthless to an agent — the general shape of
ecommerce advice is already in the model. What is *not* in the model is your
method. So the note that earns its place is a **rule that fires in a
situation**, not a description of what the lesson covered.

Weak:  "Talks about hooks and why they matter."
Strong: "First 2 seconds must show the product in use, no talking head."

Four prompts, and you will not have all four for every lesson:

1. **The rule** — what to actually do, concretely enough to follow.
2. **The trigger** — when it applies. "When a video passes 10k views", "when
   a product has had 20 posts and nothing".
3. **The number** — any threshold, count, duration or price he gives. Numbers
   are the single most useful thing here and the thing I can least guess.
4. **Disagree?** — where you think he is wrong for your niche, or where you
   have tried it and it did not hold. Mark these clearly. They override the
   lesson, and they are the most valuable notes in this file.

Anything you write as `>` blockquote I will treat as his words rather than
yours; anything under **Disagree** I will record as yours overriding his.
