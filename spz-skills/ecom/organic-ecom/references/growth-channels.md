# Beyond your own accounts: influencers, paid, and competitor intel

## The double test order — estimating a competitor's revenue

Dropped in passing in a lifestyle vlog, and the cleverest single tactic in the
corpus.

1. Place a test order on a competitor's store. Note the order number (e.g. 2115).
2. Wait a known period.
3. Place a second test order. Note the new number (e.g. 2215).
4. **The delta is their order count for that window.** Here, 100 orders.
5. Multiply by an AOV estimate for their revenue.

Shopify order numbers increment globally per store, which is what makes this work.
Costs two orders and gives a real number instead of a guess about whether a niche
is actually earning.

Related sanity check he offers: "6K per mill" (revenue per million views) is
"pretty high for a WOW product" — useful when judging whether a competitor's
claimed numbers are plausible. See `diagnostics.md` for the full band.

## Vetting an influencer or UGC creator

- **Check LAST-7-DAYS reach, never 30-day.** Thirty-day figures are inflated: his
  own best 30-day Instagram reach was ~50 million against ~16.5 million current.
- Check **average story views**, including runs of 1/2/3 back-to-back stories.
- **Check the audience's country and language against your store's language.**
  Worked rejection: a 13M-follower account with a 60% German / 30% Spanish / 10%
  English audience paired with an English store — a wasted spend regardless of
  the follower count.
- Ask for a **7-day analytics screenshot**, story views, and post likes/comments.
  Never just ask "what's your engagement".
- Red flags: withholding data, low story views relative to followers, a dead
  account, a history that does not match.
- **Stories convert best** — that is where the link sits.
- Give bullet-point talking points, not a rigid script. Review before it posts.
- Treat a story as a 24-hour test.
- When one works, move to a **recurring monthly rate and/or affiliate commission**
  rather than repeated one-offs.
- Minimum outreach volume: **100 touches/day**, preferring story/highlight replies
  over cold DMs, which land in the filtered requests folder. Open with **"Do you do
  paid promos?"** — "I want to pay you for a post" reportedly trips spam filters.
- He was scammed $400 paying a ~130k-follower account upfront via Zelle. Treat
  unverified upfront payment as a real risk.

**Judge a creator on views versus ORDER VOLUME, not views.** A creator pulling
high views with low orders gets dropped however good the raw numbers look.

## Geo-arbitrage
Take an already-proven (even saturated) product from the US/English market and
relaunch it **translated, with a native-language creator**, in another market with
purchasing power. Cited: a retro camera relaunched in German for the Swiss market
did "$14,000 in revenue" off "400,000 views" in about a week.

This is the constructive flipside of the geo-targeting diagnosis in
`diagnostics.md` — the same insight about purchasing power, used offensively.

## The organic-to-paid bridge
**Never run cold paid ads on a fresh account.** The transition that is recommended:

1. Prove a creative organically — a real winning product with real view counts.
2. Re-edit that same creative to add a CTA.
3. Run **retargeting ads** against the people who already saw it organically, and
   against an account's own non-buying followers.

Suggested threshold: once an account has roughly **10,000-100,000 followers**
built organically. One account with 150,000 followers "cleaned up" doing this.

Rationale: you are paying for high-intent audiences you already earned, not cold
traffic. Separately: with under $1,000 of ad spend most people will not succeed on
paid "unless you get lucky", and product testing on ads typically takes "three,
four or five months".

## A platform mechanic worth knowing
**Long-form vertical (60-90 seconds) is not penalised on Instagram or TikTok**,
because both count watch time the same regardless of whether the viewer plays at
1.25x, 1.5x or 2x. YouTube does not — 2x playback halves the counted watch time.

So a longer narrative-driven creative costs you nothing on the two platforms that
matter here, while it would cost you on YouTube. Instagram Reels cap at 90 seconds.

## White label, when volume justifies it
Move to a supplier or factory that also fulfils and connects directly to Shopify.
Worked example: sourced at $5, sold at $15, and up to $30-45 in higher-margin
niches such as supplements and makeup.

## Hiring
**Hire people with no prior experience in the role and train them.** His reasoning:
preventing bad habits is easier than undoing deeply rooted ones from another
company or programme.
