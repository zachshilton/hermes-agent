## The fixed points of the year

Tax year runs 6 April to 5 April. For a tax year ending 5 April:

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| 5 October following | Deadline to register for Self Assessment if newly self-employed |
| 31 October following | Paper return deadline |
| 31 January following | Online return deadline, balancing payment, and first payment on account |
| 31 July after that | Second payment on account |

Payments on account are each 50% of the previous year's tax bill, due in
advance. They surprise people in their second year of trading — the January
bill is the balancing payment *plus* the first instalment of next year's.
They can be reduced if income has genuinely fallen, but under-reducing them
attracts interest.

## What is deductible

Expenses must be **wholly and exclusively** for the business. Where something
is used both ways — phone, car, home broadband, a room at home — only the
business proportion is allowable, and the apportionment has to be defensible.

This matters for Zach's data specifically: his categories are convenience
labels over a personal current account. **A category total is not an
allowable-expense total.** Anything derived from `get_spend_by_category` is a
starting point for a conversation with an accountant, never a figure to put
on a return.

Simplified alternatives exist and are often better for small amounts: flat
mileage rates instead of actual motoring costs, and a flat monthly rate for
working from home. Whether either beats actual costs depends on his numbers.

## National Insurance and MTD

- Class 2 NICs were abolished for the self-employed from April 2024;
  voluntary contributions remain available to protect the State Pension
  record for those under the small profits threshold. Class 4 is charged on
  profits above a lower limit. **Check current rates and limits.**
- The trading allowance means very small self-employment income may not need
  reporting at all. **Check the current amount.**
- **Making Tax Digital for Income Tax began phasing in from April 2026**,
  starting with the highest qualifying income and stepping down in later
  years. If Zach's qualifying income is above the current entry threshold he
  may already be required to keep digital records and file quarterly updates
  rather than one annual return. This is the single most likely thing to have
  changed under him without notice — flag it, and point him at GOV.UK or his
  accountant rather than asserting whether he is in scope.
